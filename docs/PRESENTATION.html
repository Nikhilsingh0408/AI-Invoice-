GROUP 14 · 1 / 5

AI Invoice Auditor

Multilingual invoice validation with a durable audit trail.

Business problem

Manual review combines document reading, translation, arithmetic checks and purchase-order matching. The system automates these steps and routes exceptions to an auditor.

Outcome: approved, rejected or awaiting human review, with recorded evidence.

DEPLOYED ARCHITECTURE · 2 / 5

Six services, clear ownership

PostgreSQL and Qdrant use named volumes. Redis is disposable. Invoice binaries remain on mounted storage. ERP access passes through its HTTP API.

PROCESSING AND CONTROL · 3 / 5

Evidence at every stage

  1. Detect and checksum; skip previously audited content.
  2. Extract and retain text; translate when required.
  3. Parse typed fields and validate against rules and ERP records.
  4. Persist findings and reports; pause for human review when needed.
  5. Index completed invoices and reports for retrieval.

Corrections are new audit rows. Revalidation preserves original extracted values.

QUERYING AND IMPLEMENTATION · 4 / 5

Grounded answers and explicit limits

  • Structured questions use PostgreSQL context; semantic questions retrieve Qdrant chunks.
  • Reflection evaluates relevance, groundedness and context relevance.
  • Redis caches model and query results. RAGAS provides additional evaluation.
  • ChatBedrock is retained by project decision; LiteLLM is a documented specification deviation.

Review acceptance and retry behavior against the requirements matrix before claiming complete compliance.

VERIFICATION AND OPERATIONS · 5 / 5

Demonstrate durability, not just startup

  • Show six healthy services and service-name resolution.
  • Process an invoice and inspect its report, audit rows and vectors.
  • Recreate containers: retained volumes preserve the records.
  • Delete test volumes: the isolated stores become empty.
  • Inspect image users, sizes and build history; exclude credentials from submission.

Use the verification JSON and requirements matrix as evidence. Synthetic storage checks do not replace live invoice, Bedrock or dashboard demonstrations.
